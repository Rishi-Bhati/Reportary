Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you register for Reportary, we collect your email address, chosen username, and any profile information you optionally provide (such as your name, GitHub link, or organisation affiliation).

When you use the platform, we collect usage data including reports you create, comments you post, projects you join, and actions you take (audit logs). This data is essential for core functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Reportary platform
  • To send transactional emails (email verification, password resets, invitation notifications)
  • To support collaboration features (project invitations, @mentions)
  • To maintain audit trails for security and accountability
  • To respond to support requests submitted via our contact form

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit. Passwords are hashed using Django's PBKDF2 algorithm and are never stored in plaintext.

Access to project data is strictly controlled: users can only access projects they own, are members of, or that are explicitly marked as public.

4. File Uploads & Attachments

Report attachments you upload are stored via Cloudinary, our cloud media provider. Files are served over HTTPS. You may delete attachments at any time from within the platform.

5. Cookies & Sessions

Reportary uses session cookies strictly for authentication (keeping you logged in) and for transient state such as recent search history and recently viewed reports. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You may request deletion of your account at any time from your Edit Profile page. Upon deletion:

  • Your account is immediately deactivated
  • It is permanently and irreversibly deleted after 30 days
  • You may reactivate it by logging in before the 30-day window expires
  • Anonymised audit log entries may be retained to preserve project integrity

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Reportary:

  • Cloudinary — file and media storage
  • Gmail SMTP — transactional email delivery

These providers have their own privacy policies and are used solely to support the platform's operation.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us. We will respond within 5 business days.
