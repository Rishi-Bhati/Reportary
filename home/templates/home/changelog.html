What's New in Reportary

Updates, improvements, and new features released in Phase 2.

Feature

Basic Organization Support

Engineering teams can now create organizations, invite members, and scope projects. Assign a dedicated Project Head to manage organization-owned projects with full administrative control.

Organizations Project Head Access Control
Security

3-Level Access Scoping & UUIDs

Granular permissions are now enforced across the board with three distinct visibility levels: Public, Org-Only, and Private. Furthermore, database records (projects, reports, comments) now utilize non-enumerable, secure UUIDv7 identifiers.

UUIDv7 Scoping Permissions
Audit

Report History & Audit Logs

Full history tracking for issue reports. View who updated fields (status, priority, assignment) and when. Keeps teams perfectly in sync on progress.
